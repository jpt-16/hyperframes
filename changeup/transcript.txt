This pitch makes a 95 mph fastball look even faster.
A changeup is designed to look almost identical to a fastball coming out of the pitcher’s hand.
Same arm action. Similar release. But there’s one major difference: the speed.
Because the changeup travels slower, the hitter’s brain expects the ball to arrive sooner than it actually does.
The hitter commits to the swing too early, and the ball is still traveling toward the plate.
The best changeups disguise themselves until the last possible moment.
If a hitter can’t recognize the difference early enough, their timing is completely disrupted.
That’s the secret of the changeup. It doesn’t have to overpower a hitter. It just has to make them swing at the wrong time.
