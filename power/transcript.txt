How can a smaller hitter hit a baseball harder than someone twice his size?
You might think hitting the ball harder is all about having bigger muscles.
But raw strength is only one piece of the equation.
The real secret is how efficiently a hitter transfers energy from the ground, through their legs and hips, into their upper body and eventually the bat.
Great hitters coordinate these movements in sequence.
Their hips begin rotating, their torso follows, and the bat accelerates through the hitting zone.
That’s why two hitters with similar strength can produce completely different results.
It’s not just how strong you are.
It’s how well you use that strength.
So what matters more: strength or mechanics?
