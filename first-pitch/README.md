# "Take the first pitch, or swing?"

A 28.4 s vertical (1080×1920, 30 fps) 3D baseball breakdown for Baseball Decoded: is it worth taking the first pitch, or should hitters swing early?

- **Framework:** the same three.js field, players, strike-zone graphics, split-screen renderer and HyperFrames workflow as `lefty`, `first-baseman`, `catcher` and the earlier videos.
- **Voiceover:** HyperFrames' local Kokoro TTS, voice `am_michael` at 1.45×, which fits the script to the storyboard's timing. The script is in `transcript.txt`.
- **Sync:** the shots, captions, count changes and text are keyed to its words.
- **Music:** a bed made by the shared `make_bed` generator (`jtb-shorts/lib/audio.py`). It ducks up to 7 dB under the voice and lands its chord on "Would you swing or take?".
- **Sound:** pitch whooshes, glove pops, bat contact (a full crack on the drive, a dull one on the weak contact), the call, and count pops.
- **Mixes:** mastered to about −14 LUFS. `assets/mix-novo.wav` is the music and effects at the same level, without the voice.
- **Finished composition:** `index.html` (generated by `build.py`). The 3D scene is in `assets/field.js`.

```bash
python3 tools/physics.py                                       # pitches + batted balls -> assets/play.json
export HYPERFRAMES_PYTHON=<python with kokoro-onnx, soundfile, onnx>
$HYPERFRAMES_PYTHON tools/vo.py && python3 tools/assemble.py   # voiceover + per-word timing
python3 build.py                                               # index.html + assets/mix.wav (+ mix-novo.wav)
npx --yes hyperframes@0.8.154 render . -o renders/first-pitch.mp4 --video-bitrate 16M
```

## The numbers

**~60% first-pitch strike rate:** this is the approximate MLB league average, and the voiceover says "around 60 percent".
- Recent seasons sit at about 61–62%: 61.3% in 2018 per Razzball, and an all-time high near 62.1% recently per Pitcher List.
- It varies by season, pitcher and hitter, and the on-screen note says so.
- The brief suggested "~60–65%", but nothing found supports a league average of 65%, so the video shows ~60%.
- First-pitch strikes include called, swinging and foul strikes and balls in play. The figure is not a share of pitches a hitter should swing at.
- Check the current season on FanGraphs (F-Strike%) or Baseball Savant before publishing.

**The ten pitches on the zone are an illustration:**
- They come from `tools/physics.py`: a right-handed pitcher's fastballs, sliders, changeups and curves, aimed at chosen spots.
- Each is labeled strike or ball by where it really crosses the front of the plate: the 17 in plate plus the ball's radius, and 1.6–3.4 ft high.
- 6 of the 10 are strikes.

**The batted balls** have gravity, drag and spin, and bounces that keep half their vertical speed:
- **The drive:** a 103 mph line drive at 14° into the left-center gap. It first lands about 346 ft out, so it's not a home run.
- **The weak contact:** 62 mph, topped into the dirt toward second.
