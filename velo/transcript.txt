How does a human being throw a baseball over 100 miles per hour?
It doesn’t happen from the arm alone.
A pitcher’s delivery involves the entire body, starting with how they interact with the ground.
As the delivery progresses, the pelvis and torso rotate in a coordinated sequence.
The timing of these movements plays an important role in how the body generates and transfers energy.
Then comes the explosive arm action.
The shoulder rotates internally, the elbow extends, and the hand accelerates toward release.
These movements happen incredibly quickly.
At release, all of these movements have contributed to producing an incredibly fast-moving baseball.
And the entire delivery happens in a fraction of a second.
And that’s the biomechanics behind an elite fastball.
