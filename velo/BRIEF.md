# Baseball Decoded: "Why do some pitchers throw 100 mph?"

## Intent
A premium 3D explainer of an elite fastball delivery. Six scenes: the hook (ball stopped midair, 100 mph gauge), the lower body,
pelvis and trunk rotation, the arm (external rotation, internal rotation, elbow extension), the release and the catch, the end card.

## Assets
- The Baseball Decoded series kit (three.js ballpark, geometric players, fonts, sound effects), copied from `bat-speed`.
- Voiceover: local Kokoro TTS, voice `am_michael`. No paid generators.

## Customizations
- Series style kept (the user's choice on `bat-speed`): geometric figures, so no facial detail.
- Built in one go, no sketch pass (the user's standing preference from `bat-speed`).

## Notes: accuracy guardrails (from the user)
- No implication that the lower body simply transfers its energy into the arm; the narration and visuals show a coordinated sequence.
- Shoulder external rotation, internal rotation and elbow extension are shown and labelled separately.
- No invented statistics, forces or contributions; the force arrows and the delivery are labelled as illustrations.
- On-screen note: timing and mechanics vary from pitcher to pitcher.
