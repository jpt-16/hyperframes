# Storyboard: What actually creates bat speed?

This video tells serious players, coaches and fans that **bat speed comes from the whole body working together, not one part**.

Two settings: the series' **night ballpark** for the real swing (hook, contact, home run), and a dark **biomechanics lab**
(grid floor, a force plate under each foot, capture-camera markers) for the teaching shots. The same hitter is used everywhere.
Segment colors are kept throughout: **legs/ground amber · pelvis orange · torso cyan · arms/hands green · bat/barrel white-gold**.

| # | Time | Beat | On screen | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | 0–5 s | Hook | Ballpark, tight on the hands and bat. The swing explodes, then freezes just before contact. The uniform fades to x-ray and the colored segments light up. **WHAT ACTUALLY CREATES BAT SPEED?** | Sets up the question. The freeze and x-ray promise we'll look inside the swing. |
| 02 | 5–13 s | Lower body | Lab, low front ¾ on the legs. He loads into his back leg. Each foot sits on a force plate with an arrow showing the direction and relative size of the push (no numbers). A support bar shows weight moving back, then onto the lead leg. The pelvis ring starts to turn. **GROUND REACTION FORCES** | The swing starts at the ground. The arrows stay at the feet, so nothing suggests force "travels" into the bat. |
| 03 | 13–22 s | Pelvis → torso | Split vertically: side view on top, overhead below. Orange pelvis bar and cyan shoulder bar rotate in slow motion, then freeze when the pelvis is open and the torso is still closed (the gap shaded). A small qualitative trace shows pelvis turning, then torso (no axis numbers). **PELVIS → TORSO** | The coordinated sequence and its timing, shown as movement. |
| 04 | 22–33 s | Arms, hands, barrel | Upper body: green arms, hands and wrists. The barrel trail brightens as it speeds up through the zone. A close-up of the gloved hands and wrists turning the barrel. Then contact with the ball. **BARREL ACCELERATION** | Corrects "it's all the hips": the arms and hands accelerate and steer the barrel. |
| 05 | 33–42 s | All together | The full swing at full speed in the ballpark, then x-ray. Segments light up in order: legs → pelvis → torso → arms → bat. Contact, then a tracking shot of the ball into the outfield. **THE ENTIRE BODY WORKS TOGETHER.** Small note: *Sequence and timing vary from hitter to hitter.* | The payoff that ties every segment together, with the honest caveat. |
| 06 | 42–45 s | Series end | Slow-motion finish, freeze on a cinematic frame, fade to the Baseball Decoded mark. **FOLLOW ALONG FOR OUR BIOMECHANICS SERIES** / BASEBALL DECODED | A short series close in the series' identity. |

Captions run word by word at the top throughout. The voiceover is the user's script, word for word.
