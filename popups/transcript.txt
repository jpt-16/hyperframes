Catching a pop-up might be one of the hardest plays for a catcher.
The ball can drift toward the dugout, backstop, or even toward the mound.
The catcher has to judge its direction while moving around the plate.
They also have to deal with their gear, avoid obstacles, and position themselves underneath the ball.
And here’s something people don’t think about: these plays are surprisingly difficult to practice.
Every pop-up is different.
The ball can drift in completely different directions, and the catcher has to react instantly.
You can’t perfectly recreate every game situation in practice.
So catching a pop-up isn’t just about catching a ball.
It’s about finding it, getting into position, and making the play in seconds.
