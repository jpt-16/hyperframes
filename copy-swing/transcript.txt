The MLB swing you’re trying to copy might be the exact reason you’re struggling to hit.
Think about it.
Professional hitters have spent years developing swings that work for their bodies, strengths, mobility, and timing.
But when a young hitter tries to copy every movement, they can end up forcing positions that don’t work naturally for them.
Look at professional hitters.
Some use big leg kicks. Others barely lift their front foot. Some have more movement in their hands, while others keep everything relatively quiet.
Yet they can all produce incredible results.
That’s because there’s no single perfect swing.
The goal isn’t to swing exactly like your favorite MLB player.
It’s to understand WHY their swing works, and figure out what works best for you.
So stop trying to become the next version of someone else. Build a swing that works for you.
Whose swing would you want to learn from?
