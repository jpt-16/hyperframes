# Baseball Decoded: "Why copying an MLB swing can hurt your hitting"

## Intent
A young hitter copies an exaggerated leg kick, loses his balance and mis-hits; three pros hit with three different movements; the
point is to learn why a swing works and build one that fits. Six scenes on the user's structure; the read sets the scene starts.

## Assets
- The Baseball Decoded series kit (field, geometric players, builds), copied from `power`. A film room with three screens was built for this episode.
- Voiceover: local Kokoro TTS, voice `am_michael`, continuous with no gaps. No paid generators.

## Customizations
- Series geometric style; built in one go. Top/bottom split screens for comparisons (they keep the figures large).

## Notes: accuracy guardrails
- Five hitters on one rig. The swings are illustrations of differing styles, not motion capture of any real player. No speeds are shown.
- The balance marker (his weight over his base) is labelled as an illustration on screen.
- No claim that any style is better; the video says there's no single perfect swing.
