What would happen if an MLB hitter stopped swinging a bat for three entire months?
At first, probably not much.
Years of experience don’t suddenly disappear.
His swing mechanics and muscle memory are still there.
But hitting isn’t just about knowing how to swing.
It’s about timing a pitch, recognizing its movement, and coordinating your entire body to make contact.
Without actually swinging, that timing and coordination could become less sharp.
Even an elite hitter could struggle to adjust to major-league velocity and breaking pitches when he first returns.
He wouldn’t suddenly forget how to hit.
But he’d likely need time in the batting cage and against live pitching to get his timing back.
Because at the major-league level, even a fraction of a second can make the difference between a home run and a strikeout.
