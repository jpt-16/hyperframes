# "First base is harder than it looks"

A 40 s vertical (1080×1920, 30 fps) 3D baseball explainer for Baseball Decoded. It covers the first baseman's stretch, saving bad throws, finishing plays for his infielders, and the bat.

- **Framework:** the same three.js field, players and HyperFrames workflow as `lefty`, `catcher`, `ground-balls`, `first-base` and the earlier videos.
- **No narration:** the video has none. The voiceover is recorded separately and laid on top.
- **Sound:** effects only (throws, glove pops, the short hop and the scoop, the runner's foot on the bag, the bat crack with the crowd rising, cut whooshes, title hits). They're mastered to −20 LUFS so a voiceover at normal level sits on top. The effects track is `assets/mix.wav`.
- **Finished composition:** `index.html` (generated by `build.py`). The 3D scene and its animation are in `assets/field.js`.

```bash
python3 tools/physics.py        # the home run -> assets/play.json
python3 build.py                # index.html + assets/mix.wav
npx --yes hyperframes@0.8.154 render . -o renders/first-baseman.mp4 --video-bitrate 16M
```

To lay your voiceover in afterwards and keep the effects under it, use any editor, or run `ffmpeg -i renders/first-baseman.mp4 -i vo.wav -filter_complex "[0:a][1:a]amix=inputs=2:normalize=0[a]" -map 0:v -map "[a]" -c:v copy -c:a aac first-baseman-vo.mp4`.

## The model (`assets/field.js`, `tools/physics.py`)

**Throws:**
- Every throw is worked out from the 3D figures themselves. It starts at the fielder's throwing hand at release and ends at the first baseman's glove at the catch, so ball and glove meet exactly.
- Speeds are 66–84 mph, under gravity.
- **Short hops:** the ball comes down into the dirt a few feet in front of him and bounces up into the glove, keeping about 40–50% of its vertical speed.
- **The missed short hop:** the "stays tall" first baseman gets the same throw. The ball carries on past him, bounces again and rolls into foul ground.

**The first baseman:**
- His right foot is pinned to the bag every frame.
- His legs are solved with two-bone IK from his pelvis height and where each foot is planted, so both feet stay on the dirt. A full stretch is about a 4 ft split with the front knee bent.
- He reads the throw and commits to the stretch about 0.35 s before the ball arrives. He never leaves the bag early on a force play.
- **The one exception is the wide throw:** he comes off the bag to keep it in front, and that play is called safe.
- **Stretch gain:** the replay's "+N ft" is measured on the figure. It compares his glove at the catch with his glove reaching from standing tall, both with the same foot on the bag.

**The home run:**
- 106 mph at 24°, pulled to left-center, flown with gravity, drag and spin lift.
- It clears the 8 ft wall about 33 ft up at 380 ft and lands in the seats at about 421 ft.

**The batter-runner:** he accelerates out of the box and runs through the bag, reaching it a step before or after the catch, as each play needs.
