Why would a manager intentionally put the other team's best hitter on base?
Because sometimes, the real target isn't the hitter at the plate. It's the matchup that comes next.
But there's a risk.
In the 2024 NLCS, the Dodgers intentionally walked Francisco Lindor. The next batter, Mark Vientos, responded with a grand slam.
That's the gamble: avoid one dangerous hitter, but give another a chance with runners on base.
Analytics says the extra baserunner matters. So managers have to weigh the hitter, the next matchup, the outs, and whether a double play is possible.
So when is walking a superstar actually smart baseball?
