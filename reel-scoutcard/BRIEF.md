## Intent

Founder-journey Reel introducing ScoutCard AI. Captions throughout plus
content-driven graphics: the abstract ideas he talks through get drawn
(the founder curve, the CV tracking he dropped), the product itself is shown
from a real screen recording.

## Assets

- base_tight.mp4 — six clips (IMG_1778, 1781, 1784, 1785, 1789, 1795),
  normalised to -14 LUFS and tone-mapped HLG->Rec.709, concatenated in
  narrative order (filename order, verified against content), 3.85s of pauses
  cut, re-normalised.
- transcript.json / anchors.json / cues.json — 320 words with onsets; every
  graphic and SFX hit is pinned to an anchor word. "Huddle" corrected to
  "Hudl" throughout (ASR mishears the platform name).
- assets/scoutcard-broll.mp4 — the user's ScoutCard AI screen recording: iOS
  status bar cropped, fit to 9:16 keeping the logo header, slowed
  10.35s -> 17.0s (0.61x) so headlines are readable. Plays 29.2-46.2.
- assets/scoutcard-logo*.png — the real logo lifted from the recording's own
  header, not recreated.
- build.py — generates index.html from the transcript. Edit it, not the HTML.
