Tommy John surgery doesn't magically make pitchers throw harder.
It rebuilds the ligament that stabilizes your elbow during a throw.
And here's the surprising part: a study of MLB pitchers found that only 28% returned to full performance within three seasons of surgery.
Getting back on the mound and getting back to your old level are two completely different things.
Recovery can take 12 to 18 months, and even after that, pitchers still need time to regain their command and performance.
The surgery can save a career. But it doesn't guarantee you'll get your old arm back.
