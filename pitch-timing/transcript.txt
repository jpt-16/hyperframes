Why do pitchers constantly change how long they take to deliver the ball?
Because runners are watching their every move.
When a pitcher repeatedly uses the same timing, baserunners can recognize patterns and anticipate exactly when the ball is coming.
This gives them a better opportunity to steal a base.
That's why pitchers mix things up.
Sometimes they'll use a quick delivery, other times they'll take longer, and they'll vary their pickoff moves to keep runners guessing.
But what happens if a pitcher doesn't change their timing?
Runners can start getting bigger jumps, stealing bases more easily, and putting pressure on the defense.
At higher levels, even a tiny timing advantage can make the difference between safe and out.
In baseball, pitchers aren't just competing against hitters. They're competing against the runners, too.
