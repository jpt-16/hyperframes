A balk isn't always caused by the pitcher making a mistake.
Here are five different ways a balk can be called.
Number one: The pitcher starts his delivery, then stops before throwing the ball.
Number two: The pitcher comes set but doesn't come to a complete stop before delivering the pitch.
Number three: The pitcher throws to first without stepping directly toward the base.
Number four: The pitcher drops the ball while touching the pitching rubber.
And number five: The catcher balk.
Yes, the catcher can actually cause a balk.
If the catcher steps outside the catcher's box before the pitcher releases the ball, it's a violation.
And there's one more important detail: a balk generally requires runners on base, and the penalty is that runners advance one base.
Baseball has some rules that even experienced fans don't know.
