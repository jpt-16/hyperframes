Everyone talks about how difficult it is to play shortstop.
But what if the hardest position in baseball is actually catcher?
Think about it.
Every single pitch, the catcher has to receive a ball traveling at incredible speeds, sometimes with movement that makes it difficult to handle cleanly.
If a pitch bounces in the dirt, they have to block it and keep the ball from getting past them.
If a runner tries to steal, they have to get out of their stance, transfer the ball, and make an accurate throw to second base in seconds.
But there’s another part people forget.
The catcher is also responsible for communicating with the pitcher, helping manage the defense, and making constant decisions about what’s happening on the field.
And they have to do all of this while wearing heavy protective equipment and staying focused for the entire game.
So, is catcher actually the hardest position in baseball?
Or does another position deserve that title?
