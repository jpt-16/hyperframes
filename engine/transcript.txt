  0.20  Second base looks easy… until you see everything these players have to do.
  3.06  Number one: turning double plays. They have to catch the ball, position their feet, avoid the incoming runner, and get the throw off almost instantly.
  9.27  Number two: range. Second basemen have to cover ground up the middle, react quickly, and turn balls that look like hits into outs.
 15.28  Number three: communication. They’re constantly working with the shortstop to decide who’s covering second, who’s taking the ball, and where the next throw goes.
 21.96  And number four: the throws. They constantly have to throw accurately from awkward angles, while moving, or right after making a difficult play.
 28.05  Second base isn’t just about fielding ground balls.
 30.33  It’s about making the entire infield work.
